Connect Bluesky

Create an App Password in Bluesky (Settings → Privacy & Security → App Passwords) and enter it here. It is stored only in FeedEcho's local database. App passwords can post on your behalf but cannot change account settings or log in to the Bluesky app.

Connect Micro.blog

Create an App Token at micro.blog (Account → Edit Apps → Add Token, or micro.blog/account/apps) and paste it here. FeedEcho discovers every blog the token can post to and connects each one. Tokens can post on your behalf but cannot change account settings.
