Features

Every destination

Mastodon, Bluesky, micro.blog, Matrix, and email — connect each once, target any of them per feed.

Your voice, templated

Jinja2 templates shape each post: titles, links, summaries, truncation — your format, not ours.

Filters and warnings

Keyword filters include or exclude items; optional content warnings hold posts behind a spoiler.

Drip or digest

Rate-limit bursts with drip posting, or batch a whole day into one tidy email digest.

Accessible images

Feed images attach automatically, with AI-generated alt text when you enable it.

Reliable delivery

Bounded retries with backoff, duplicate protection, and email alerts when a feed breaks.

How it works

  1. Connect a destination. Authorize Mastodon, add a Bluesky app password, a micro.blog token, a Matrix access token and room, or your SMTP details.
  2. Add a feed. Any RSS, Atom, or JSON feed — set how often to check it.
  3. Create an echo. A template that turns new items into posts, with optional filters, images, and pacing.
